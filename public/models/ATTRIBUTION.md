## Modifications

Optimized using glTF Transform 4.5.1: deduplication/joining, Meshopt geometry compression (original geometry retained; no simplification), and WebP textures resized to at most 1024 pixels. Separate materials retained. The application normalizes scale, adjusts paint/glass materials, and adds independent conceptual thruster effects. The effects are not part of the original model and do not represent engineering specifications.

This is an independent interactive concept study, not an official Tesla website. Tesla and Roadster names identify the subject of the model.
